# IT Troubleshooting Guide: VPN and Passwords

## Resetting your VPN
1. Open the VPN client and click Disconnect.
2. Go to the self-service portal at id.company.example and choose "Reset VPN token".
3. Approve the request on your registered mobile authenticator app.
4. Restart the VPN client and sign in with the new token.
If the token still fails after 3 attempts, raise a ticket with the IT helpdesk (extension 4000, helpdesk@company.example).

## Forgotten password
Use the self-service portal and select "Forgot password". Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and are rotated every 90 days.
Never share your password or authenticator codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## Laptop issues
For a slow or frozen laptop, restart it first. If the problem continues, raise an IT ticket. Hardware replacements take 3 working days.
