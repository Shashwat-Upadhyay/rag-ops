# HR Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the line manager.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Days beyond 10 expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
Inform your manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of sickness.

## Parental leave
Mothers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth.

## Public holidays
The company follows the official public holiday calendar published every January. Employees working on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off.
