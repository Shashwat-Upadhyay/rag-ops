# Finance: Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Limits
Employees may claim up to 5000 rupees per day for domestic travel meals and local transport.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 8000 rupees per night in metro cities and 5000 rupees per night elsewhere.
Any single expense above 20000 rupees requires prior approval from the department head.

## How to claim
Submit the claim in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, attaching original receipts.
Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless the CFO approves an exception.
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary cycle.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and personal shopping are not reimbursable.
