# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Week 1
On day one, collect your laptop and access card from the reception desk and complete the HR joining formalities.
You will receive a buddy who helps you settle in. IT creates your email and VPN accounts before your joining date.

## First month
Complete the mandatory security awareness training and the code of conduct course within 30 days.
Meet your manager weekly to agree your 30-60-90 day goals.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months. A performance review is held at the end of month 3 and month 6.
Employees are confirmed in writing after a successful final review.
